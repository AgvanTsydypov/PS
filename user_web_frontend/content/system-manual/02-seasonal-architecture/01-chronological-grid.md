The ecosystem runs on a dual-track architecture, supporting two distinct temporal frameworks simultaneously.

**THE STANDARD CYCLE (Season 1, 2, N...)** Standard deployments enforce a strict **10-Day Minimum Cycle**. This creates a predictable, unyielding rhythm for systemic extraction.

* **The Active Window (Days 1–9):** The ledger is unlocked. Stars are claimable based on phase restrictions.
* **The Ghost State (Early Depletion):** If 100% of the structural supply is claimed before Day 9, the network enters a dormant "Ghost State." It waits silently until the 9-day clock expires.
* **The Hard Stop (Time Expiration):** If unclaimed Stars remain at the stroke of Day 9, the remaining slots are permanently deleted. The Season closes.
* **The Transmission (Day 10):** A mandatory 24-hour data uplink period before the next Season initializes.

**THE GENESIS EPOCH** Genesis is the foundational, macro-historical event that boots the entire ecosystem. It possesses a hardcoded supply of 2,000 Stars.

* **Unbound Duration:** Genesis possesses no temporal ceiling. It remains active until 100% of its Stars are claimed.
* **Parallel Execution:** The ecosystem does not wait for Genesis to close. Standard Season 1 will commence on its exact scheduled launch date (10 days after Genesis commenced), regardless of whether the 2,000 Genesis Stars have been fully depleted.
* **Unblocking State:** If Genesis Stars remain unclaimed when a Standard Season boots, Genesis simply remains open, running seamlessly in parallel with the active Standard Cycle until its supply hits zero.
