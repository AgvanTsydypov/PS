Every Season navigates through four distinct systemic phases. The network dynamically advances to the next phase when either the **Time Limit** expires or the **Supply Cap** is triggered.

⚔️ **PHASE 1: THE BREACH (Days 1–3)** The initial kinetic rush. The network is vulnerable to all actors.
* **Access Level:** GLOBAL (Origins & Looters).
* **Supply Ceiling:** 20% of Total Seasonal Pool.
* **Termination Trigger:** 72 hours elapse **OR** the 20% cap is shattered.
* *System Note: If the 20% cap is consumed in hour one, The Breach seals immediately.*

🛡️ **PHASE 2: THE VAULT (Days 4–6)** The cryptographic lockdown. Extraction is restricted strictly to mathematically verified participants.
* **Access Level:** RESTRICTED (Origins Only).
* **Supply Ceiling:** Remaining Supply.
* **Termination Trigger:** 72 hours elapse **OR** 100% pool depletion.
* *System Note: Secondary market actors (Looters) are locked out and must await The Scavenge.*

💀 **PHASE 3: THE SCAVENGE (Days 7–9)** The final sweep. All structural restrictions are lifted.
* **Access Level:** GLOBAL (Origins & Looters).
* **Supply Ceiling:** Remaining Supply.
* **Termination Trigger:** 100% pool depletion **OR** Day 9 expires.
* *System Note: Any Stars surviving this phase face immediate systemic incineration.*

📡 **PHASE 4: THE TRANSMISSION (24 Hours)** The network reboot.
* **Access Level:** ZERO ACCESS.
* **Supply Ceiling:** ZERO SUPPLY.
* **Termination Trigger:** 24-hour exact countdown.
* *System Note: The ledger is closed. The telemetry pipeline is actively ingesting resolved data to forge the deck for the ensuing Season.*
