## 2.1 Overview

Every PS card carries an **Archetype**: a categorical classification of the wallet's behavior on the underlying event. Archetypes are not titles, ranks, or qualitative judgments. They are the terminal output of a deterministic classification engine that processes each wallet's event-level performance through a fixed sequence of mathematical gates.

> The system recognizes **thirteen Archetypes**. Their distribution across the ecosystem is not enforced by quota; it emerges from the underlying behavioral data of the Polymarket ledger. Empirical scarcity ranges from a structural baseline of approximately **44% (SUBSTRATE)** to apex tiers occupying less than **0.3% of the population (INSIDER)**.

Classification is governed by two architectural principles:

* **Hybrid Matrix:** The system combines absolute financial gates (fiat thresholds, PnL state) with relative percentile rankings (intra-event cohort comparisons). Absolute gates are evaluated first; relative percentiles are computed only on the surviving population.
* **Priority Waterfall:** Archetypes are resolved in a strict, deterministic order. A wallet matching multiple criteria receives the highest-priority match; all subsequent evaluations are skipped. The complete waterfall is published in Section 7.3 to permit independent reconstruction of any classification from raw event data.

## 2.2 The Four Axes

Each wallet's behavior is measured along four orthogonal axes. Three are derived dynamically from the event population; one is anchored to absolute market state at the moment of capital deployment.

#### Axis 1. Entry Bracket: $P(E)$
The Entry Bracket records the implied probability of the event at the moment the wallet's capital was deployed. It is computed from the **Capital-Weighted Average Price (CWAP)** of the wallet's position and expressed as a static interval. The bracket is absolute: it does not depend on other participants. It is a permanent record of the probabilistic landscape the trader chose to enter.

| Bracket | Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **[0.00 – 0.20]** | Pre-narrative deployment. Capital executed before consensus formation. |
| **[0.20 – 0.40]** | Deep contrarian foresight. Position taken against forming consensus. |
| **[0.40 – 0.60]** | Maximal entropy. The statistical coin-flip zone. |
| **[0.60 – 0.80]** | Trend reinforcement. Capital aligned with directional momentum. |
| **[0.80 – 0.97]** | Pre-terminal consensus. Late-stage participation prior to full crystallization. |
| **[0.97 – 1.00]** | Terminal certainty. Capital deployed when the outcome is functionally resolved. |

#### Axis 2. Edge: Relative Timing
**Edge** measures how early the wallet established its position relative to other participants in the same event. Computed via `PERCENT_RANK()` over CWAP, partitioned by event. Edge ranks the wallet against its immediate cohort, not the global population.

#### Axis 3. Yield: Capital Efficiency
**Yield** measures the ROI generated by the wallet's deployed capital: realized PnL divided by total dollars deployed. Computed via `PERCENT_RANK()` over ROI, partitioned by event. Yield is the ranking of skill, normalized for capital size:  a wallet that doubled its investment on a rather small amount ranks above a wallet that made 10% on **$40,000.00**.

> **Note:** Yield is computed only on wallets surviving the absolute profitability gate. Wallets with negative or zero PnL are quarantined upstream and never enter the Yield ranking.

#### Axis 4. Gravity: Capital Footprint
**Gravity** measures the absolute fiat mass deployed by the wallet: total USD at risk on the event. Computed via `PERCENT_RANK()` over total volume, partitioned by event. Gravity is the ranking of influence: how much capital the wallet committed to bend the local market structure.

Gravity is governed by absolute kinetic floors:
* Wallets deploying under **$50.00** cannot reach the upper Gravity tiers regardless of their relative ranking.
* Wallets deploying **$5,000.00** or more at terminal certainty are routed to a separate classification entirely.
* These floors are evaluated before the percentile computation and are detailed in Section 7.3.

## 2.3 The Priority Waterfall

The waterfall is the deterministic resolution order through which every wallet is processed. Evaluation halts at the first matching gate; all lower-priority Archetypes are not considered for that wallet.

> The architecture is organized into three layers reflecting evaluation order, not rarity. Layer numbering is a property of the algorithm, not a ranking of prestige. A **Layer 1** classification (ICARUS, BURNER, BOT) may be empirically rarer than a **Layer 3** classification (OPERATOR, GRAVITON), or vice versa. Behavioral frequencies are independent of layer.

#### Layer 1: Quarantine
Wallets are first evaluated against absolute mathematical conditions. These gates remove distortions before relative ranking begins.

| Order | Archetype | Gate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **ICARUS** | $$P(E) < 0.60 \land PnL < \$0.00$$ |
| **2** | **BURNER** | $$P(E) \geq 0.60 \land PnL < \$0.00$$ |
| **3** | **BOT** | $$PnL \equiv \$0.00$$ |

* **ICARUS** captures early-conviction failure: capital deployed against forming consensus, ledger settled against the thesis. 
* **BURNER** captures late-stage exit liquidity: capital deployed into mature consensus that subsequently inverted. 
* **BOT** captures algorithmic neutrality: zero net directional outcome, the mathematical signature of arbitrage routing or token-merge activity rather than predictive participation.

#### Layer 2: Consensus
Wallets surviving Layer 1 are evaluated for terminal-certainty behavior. Polymarket's late-stage cohort represents **58.18%** of the platform population; routing them through dedicated classifications preserves percentile integrity for the apex layer below.

| Order | Archetype | Gate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **4** | **EXTRACTOR** | $$P(E) \geq 0.97 \land Vol \geq \$5,000.00$$ |
| **5** | **PASSENGER** | $$P(E) \geq 0.97 \land \$50.00 \leq Vol < \$5,000.00$$ |
| **6** | **SUBSTRATE** | $$P(E) \geq 0.97 \land Vol < \$50.00$$ (Terminal Branch) |

The kinetic floors at **$50** and **$5,000** separate institutional-scale yield extraction from retail consensus participation and sub-threshold dust.

#### Layer 3: Apex Core
Wallets surviving Layers 1 and 2 enter the apex layer, where **Edge**, **Yield**, and **Gravity** percentiles are evaluated. Only profitable, directional, non-terminal wallets reach this layer.

| Order | Archetype | Gate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **7** | **INSIDER** | $P(E) \leq 0.10 \land Yield \geq P99$ |
| **8** | **ANOMALY** | One of the three coherence tuples (see below) |
| **9** | **SIGNAL** | $P(E) \in [0.00 – 0.40] \land Edge \geq P90 \land Yield \geq P90$ |
| **10** | **VECTOR** | $P(E) \in [0.40 – 0.60] \land Edge \geq P90 \land Yield \geq P90$ |
| **11** | **EQUILIBRIUM** | $Edge \geq P70 \land Yield \geq P70 \land Gravity \geq P70$ |
| **12** | **GRAVITON** | $Gravity \geq P90$ |
| **13** | **SUBSTRATE** | $P(E) \in [0.60 – 0.97]$, $Edge/Yield \in \{Base, P50\}$, $Gravity \in \{Base, P50, P70\}$ |
| **14** | **OPERATOR** | Default classification — surviving wallet matching no prior gate |

#### The ANOMALY Coherence Tuples
**ANOMALY** identifies wallets whose Edge, Yield, and Gravity align at a percentile depth corresponding to their probabilistic terrain. The deeper the entry, the higher the required tier across all three axes.

| Entry Bracket | Required Edge | Required Yield | Required Gravity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[0.00 – 0.20]** | $P99$ | $P99$ | $P99$ |
| **[0.20 – 0.40]** | $P90$ | $P90$ | $P90$ |
| **[0.40 – 0.60]** | $P70$ | $P70$ | $P70$ |

> The **[0.60 – 1.00]** brackets are excluded. Coherence is a property of capital deployed into **uncertainty**, not into trend or pre-consensus.

### Notes on Edge Cases

* **OPERATOR** is the default fallback. It captures the standard middle-of-distribution participant: the card-display gate Edge ⇌ Yield | min(EYG) ≤ P70 reflects this empirical signature.
* **SUBSTRATE** has two distinct routing paths:
    1.  **Layer 2 (Terminal):** Sub-$50 deployment into resolved markets.
    2.  **Layer 3 (Tail):** Wallets with mediocre performance in trend-reinforcement brackets.
    * Both terminate in the same Archetype because the underlying behavioral signature—non-influential, non-predictive participation—is structurally identical.

## 2.4 The Thirteen Archetypes. Terminal Dossiers

Each Archetype carries a dossier: the descriptive frame inscribed on the back of the card. The mathematical gate is restated; the dossier provides the behavioral interpretation.

The order below tracks empirical frequency, with one deviation: **SIGNAL** and **VECTOR** are presented adjacently so the reader can compare them as different kinds of rare rather than different levels of rare.

* **SUBSTRATE — Baseline Matter // Network Plankton**

  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~44%
   * **Math:** $$P(E) \geq 0.97 \land Vol < \$50.00 \mid \text{OR Mid-Trend Lag}$$
    
      > Structural baseline matter. Operates at the absolute kinetic floor of the ecosystem, executing entirely on lagging indicators or sweeping micro-dust at terminal certainty. Lacking both the financial mass to influence the matrix and the velocity to predict it, forms the dense, low-tier static of the network. The organic, foundational plankton that sustains the broader probabilistic food chain.

* **OPERATOR — Prevailing Wind // Decentralized Node**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~17%
  * **Math:** $$Edge \rightleftharpoons Yield \mid \min(EYG) \leq P70$$

    > Standard kinetic node. Forms the prevailing wind of the probability matrix. Executes mid-trend, absorbs average systemic risk, and generates standard baseline returns. Is not the whale anchoring the market, nor the sniper predicting it. The active, decentralized processing power of the ledger — the everyday volume that keeps the terminal alive.

* **PASSENGER — Consensus Absorption // Trailing Weight**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~14%
  * **Math:** $$P(E) \geq 0.97 \mid \$50.00 \leq Vol < \$5,000.00$$

    > Terminal consensus achieved prior to entry. Absorbs zero predictive risk, boarding the probability matrix only after the timeline has fundamentally crystallized. Driven by the psychological need to participate rather than predict, trades all capital efficiency for the absolute safety of hindsight. Does not chart the course or bend the narrative; simply the trailing biological weight of the ledger.

* **BOT — Mechanical Routing // Zero Variance**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~7%
  * **Math:** $$PnL \equiv \$0.00$$

    > Mechanical routing protocol detected. Operates with massive kinetic force but generates zero directional trajectory, executing purely on structural arbitrage and fractional spreads. Devoid of human psychology or predictive bias, exists solely to bridge conditional markets, merge underlying tokens, and enforce absolute liquidity ceilings. Does not predict the future; mathematically processes the emotions of the swarm.

* **EQUILIBRIUM — Heavyweight Alpha // Gravitational Anchor**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~4%
  * **Math:** $$Edge \geq P70 \mid Yield \geq P70 \mid Gravity \geq P70$$

    > Heavyweight alpha baseline. Possesses the predictive velocity of an early forecaster, reinforced by the financial density to unilaterally rewrite the probability matrix. Does not wait for the market to mature; capital deployment instantly forces a global repricing event. High efficiency. High mass. The gravitational anchor of the ecosystem.

* **BURNER — Terminal Liquidity // Systemic Tax**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~3%
  * **Math:** $$P(E) \geq 0.60 \mid PnL < \$0.00$$

    > Late-stage exit liquidity. Executes under the psychological duress of momentum, buying into a heavily mature consensus just before a catastrophic timeline flip. Captivated by the illusion of certainty, absorbs minimal upside potential while blindly accepting maximum downside exposure. Does not drive the market; unknowingly finances the precision of the apex tiers. The ultimate sacrificial capital.

* **SIGNAL — Early Forecaster // Pure Velocity**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~2%
  * **Math:** $$P(E) \in [0.00 - 0.40] \mid Edge \geq P90 \mid Yield \geq P90$$

    > Early probability forecaster. Deploys capital into the void before narrative formation. Lacking the gravitational mass to forcibly bend the market, relies strictly on execution velocity and extreme absolute risk. The initial spark of the probability curve, capturing massive capital efficiency through sheer predictive vision.

* **VECTOR — Calculated Divergence // Maximal Entropy**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~2%
  * **Math:** $$P(E) \in [0.40 - 0.60] \mid Edge \geq P90 \mid Yield \geq P90$$

    > The calculated divergence. Intercepts the market at the point of maximum entropy — the statistical coin-flip. Rather than adopting the herd's velocity, executes early and takes the mathematically hostile side of a forming consensus. By absorbing peak volatility and being proven violently correct, achieves immense capital efficiency. A structural disruptor.

* **GRAVITON — Kinetic Engine // Deep Liquidity**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~2%
  * **Math:** $$Gravity \geq P90$$

    > Heavy kinetic grinder. Lacks elite execution speed and generates standard capital efficiency, yet operates with massive financial density. Deploys heavy volume into the mid-trend, serving as the raw mechanical engine of the market. Does not predict the future, nor does it wait for absolute certainty. Provides the deep liquidity floors that allow the broader ecosystem to function.

* **ICARUS — Overextension // Timeline Rejection**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~1%
  * **Math:** $$P(E) < 0.60 \mid PnL < \$0.00$$

    > Early-conviction overextension. Deploys capital into the void with extreme early conviction, attempting to forge reality before the timeline achieves consensus. Driven by pure analytical hubris, absorbs maximum absolute risk but fails to align with the final ledger. Does not adapt to shifting probabilities; demands the market bends to thesis. A tragic, mathematically shattered visionary.

* **EXTRACTOR — Whale-Tier Yield // Absolute Certainty**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~1%
  * **Math:** $$P(E) \geq 0.97 \mid Vol \geq \$5,000.00$$

    > Apex-tier consensus exploitation. Exhibits extreme risk aversion, refusing to deploy overwhelming financial mass until absolute mathematical certainty has crystallized. By parking heavy capital into fully resolved timelines, brutally extracts risk-free, low-variance yield from the ecosystem's final moments. Possesses no predictive foresight, relying entirely on financial gravity to crush the remaining fractional value out of the ledger.

* **ANOMALY — Systemic Resonance // Coherent Alignment**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~0.5%
  * **Math:** $$P(E) < 0.60 \mid Edge \equiv Yield \equiv Gravity \propto 1/P(E)$$

    > Systemic resonance detected. Capital mass, execution velocity, and predictive accuracy align in algorithmic unison with the implied probability bracket of entry. The deeper the conviction, the higher the demanded tier across all three axes. A wallet whose performance pattern mirrors the mathematical structure of the market it entered. Coherence at the boundary of statistical plausibility. Resonance in place of drag.

* **INSIDER — Non-Public Foresight // Asymmetric Yield**
  * **Behavioral frequency:** ~0.2%
  * **Math:** $$P(E) \leq 0.10 \mid Yield \geq P99$$

    > Pure information asymmetry. Does not predict; knows. Deploys capital into the void before public narrative formation, entering the matrix when the implied probability is exceptionally low. By extracting maximum absolute yield on sub-10% probability, exhibits the exact mathematical signature of non-public foresight. Lethal, early, and precise.

## 2.5 Verifiability and Versioning

The complete classification logic: the four axes, the priority waterfall, the absolute gates, and the percentile computations is deterministic and reproducible. Given a wallet's trade history on a resolved event, any participant can independently compute the resulting Archetype. The classification engine retains no discretion. The system reports what the data dictates.

The waterfall ordering published above is the canonical resolution sequence. It is fixed for the duration of a season. Any future revisions are released as versioned protocol updates and applied prospectively, never retroactively. Stars minted under v1 of the classification engine remain v1 in perpetuity.
