## 3.1 Overview

A **Card** is the visual surface of a **Star**. The Star is the artifact, the on-chain token, the immutable record of behavior. The Card is what the holder sees: the rendered SVG that translates the Star's data into a readable, collectible object. The Star is delivered as a custom **ERC-721 token** on Ethereum mainnet, incorporating royalty-enforcement features standard to the **ERC-721C** extension family.

The distinction matters:
* **The Star:** Exists regardless of whether anyone looks at it.
* **The Card:** Exists to be looked at. 

> Every visual decision documented in this section serves the second function: making the Star's underlying data legible at a glance, distinct in a collection, and consistent across the thirteen Archetypes.

#### Structural Composition

The Card has two distinct faces, each serving a unique informational purpose:

* **The Front:** Carries the **Event Identity**, the **Wallet Identity**, and the **Four-Axis Behavioral Readout**. It is the primary visual identification layer.
* **The Back:** Carries the **Narrative Interpretation**: the dossier, the frequency, the statistical pattern, and the seasonal context.

## 3.2 Card Front

The front is divided into two zones, vertically. The **Image Zone** occupies the upper half; the **Data Zone** occupies the lower half. A **Title Bridge** sits on the boundary between them.

![alt](/system-manual/ASCII_CARD_FRONT.png "0.6")

#### Image Zone

The Image Zone houses the event artwork and the metadata badges that record the Card's origin and state.

* **Event Artwork.** The central visual element. Source imagery is taken from the Polymarket API. The render is generated by an internal agent and curated to maintain stylistic consistency. This is the primary recognition layer for the event.
* **Logo Overlay (top-left).** A static PS logo, identical across all Cards. 
* **Metadata Badge (top-right).** Three lines of dynamic text encoding the Card's seasonal and ownership state:
    * **Line 1 — Season:** `GENESIS` (cyan) or `STANDARD #N` (muted grey).
    * **Line 2 — Instance:** `SINGULAR` (amber) for one-offs or `FRACTAL` (blue) for recurring series.
    * **Line 3 — Ownership:** `[ ORIGIN SECURED ]` (hot pink) for the original trader or `[ LOOTER TAKEOVER ]` (green) for a Looter claim.

> The ownership line is the **only** place on the Card where claim type is surfaced explicitly. All other data remains identical regardless of the claiming path.

#### Title Bridge

The event title sits at the boundary between the Image Zone and the Data Zone, formatted as a terminal header. Titles are compressed into headline-style labels (e.g., `US PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS 2024`) to serve as market identifiers.


#### Data Zone

The Data Zone background is determined by the Archetype, with each of the thirteen classifications receiving a distinct treatment (see Section 3.5). 

* **SECTOR (primary tag).** A categorical event classifier. Colors are assigned by an Agent via HSL hue-distance constraints for maximum visual separation.
* **NODE (secondary tag).** A finer-grained classifier within a Sector, rendered in neutral grey.
* **Wallet Address.** The first thirteen characters of the Origin wallet (e.g., `0x123abc456def...`). Truncation allows for participant recognition without cluttering the UI. Color is driven by the **Edge** metric.
* **Entry Bracket.** Displayed as $$P(E) \in [CWAP \text{ Range}]$$. The color is mapped through the **Unified Tier Map** (Section 3.4).
* **Edge / Yield / Gravity.** Labeled values rendered in colors corresponding to their respective tiers ($$P50, P70, P90, P99$$). 
* **Archetype.** The assigned classification (e.g., `INSIDER`, `SIGNAL`), displayed in its signature color.
* **Polymarket Global Rank.** The wallet's all-time PnL rank on the Polymarket leaderboard, frozen at the moment of minting. It serves as a permanent tribute to the substrate platform.

## 3.3 Card Back

The back of the Card is structured as a **dossier**. To ensure visual unity, the background color matches the Archetype's Data Zone treatment from the front, and the frame color matches the Yield-driven outer border.

![alt](/system-manual/ASCII_CARD_BACK.png "0.6")

#### Event Topology

Located in the top section, this is a concise description of the underlying event generated by the **Quant agent**.
* **Purpose:** Contextualizes the prediction market for new readers.
* **Nature:** Interpretive and narrative-driven (the "what" and "why") rather than a list of mechanical resolution criteria.


#### Behavioral Signature

The middle section identifies the core of the artifact through three specific elements:

* **Archetype & Dossier:** The full description (as defined in Section 2.4).
* **Behavioral Frequency:** The empirical frequency of the pattern across the *entire* Polymarket population that registered redemptions within the events. 
    > **Note:** This value represents how often the behavior occurs in the population (e.g., **~0.2%** for **INSIDER**), not the percentage of minted Stars.
* **Statistical Pattern:** The precise mathematical gate satisfied by the wallet, expressed in compact notation (e.g., $$P(E) \geq 0.97 \land Vol \geq \$5,000.00$$ for **EXTRACTOR**).

Together, these provide the interpretation, the rarity context, and the mathematical proof of the Card's identity.


#### Signature Composition & Seasonal Context

The bottom section contains three lines of high-density metadata:

1.  **Signature Composition:** A unique fingerprint encoded as:
    `[ARCH]-[P(E)]-[EYG]-[INST]-[CLAIM]-[SEASON]-[PLATFORM][EVENT_ID]`
    This string allows for the full reconstruction of the Card's data dimensions from the signature alone.
2.  **Season Card Number:** The Card’s specific position within the seasonal supply (e.g., `214 / 333` or `1247 / 2000` for Genesis).
3.  **Season Dates:** The immutable temporal window recording when the Card was minted (e.g., `05.06.2026 - 15.06.2026`).


#### QR Code

Positioned in the lower-right, this code resolves to a personalized page on the **PS website**. It serves as an access point to extended records, including:
* Full signature decoding.
* Detailed claim provenance.
* Extended event coverage.

## 3.4 The Propagation System

The Card's color logic is governed by a **Unified Tier Map**: a single-color palette that applies to two parallel readouts—the **Entry Bracket** and the three percentile axes (**Edge, Yield, Gravity**).

| Color | Entry Bracket $P(E)$ | Percentile Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spectrum Gradient** | $0.00 – 0.20$ | $P99$ |
| **Gold** | $0.20 – 0.40$ | $P90$ |
| **Blue** | $0.40 – 0.60$ | $P70$ |
| **Green** | $0.60 – 0.80$ | $P50$ |
| **White** | $0.80 – 0.97$ | $Base$ |
| **White** | $0.97 – 1.00$ | — |

The Entry Bracket contains six positions to map absolute implied probability across the full $$[0, 1]$$ interval. In contrast, the percentile axes carry five tiers to partition the ranked population. Note that the **terminal-certainty bracket $$[0.97 – 1.00]$$** has no percentile counterpart; it represents a mode where deployment is no longer predictive and is routed to **Layer 2 Consensus Archetypes**.

#### Visual Legibility & Propagation

The result is a "glanceable" system. A reader familiar with the palette can parse a Card’s depth and performance without reading text. A **spectrum-gradient** always denotes top-tier performance, whether it’s a deep contrarian entry ($$0.00–0.20$$) or elite relative ranking ($$P99$$).

The tier-color propagates to specific structural elements of the Card, embedding the behavioral metrics into the architecture itself:

| Metric | Element it Colors | Visual Intent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Entry Bracket** | Bracket text | Self-contained probability state. |
| **Edge** | Wallet Address text | Reads as "who got there first." |
| **Yield** | Outer Card frame | The most prominent element reflects efficiency. |
| **Gravity** | Separator line | Places capital mass as a "weight" at the base. |

* **Coherent Visuals:** A Card with high values across all axes appears unified, with gold or spectrum tones repeating across structural elements.
* **Layered Profiles:** Mixed performance results in a multi-colored object, visually encoding the wallet’s unique behavioral profile.

## 3.5 Archetype Visual Vocabulary

The Data Zone background, and by extension the back of the Card, is determined by the **Archetype**. Each of the thirteen Archetypes carries a distinct visual treatment chosen to align with its behavioral lore.

| Archetype | Treatment | Behavioral Alignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **INSIDER** | Solid dark grey/black | The opacity of unknowable foresight. |
| **ANOMALY** | Iridescent holographic gradient: cyan, magenta, yellow, green | The visual signature of mathematical resonance: spectrum behavior on a single surface. |
| **ICARUS** | Warm fiery gradient: deep reddish-orange to bright gold/yellow | Solar fall. |
| **EXTRACTOR** | Cool luminous gradient: dark blue to bright cyan/aquamarine | The cold light of late-stage certainty. |
| **SIGNAL** | Dark teal/cyan gradient deepening top to bottom | Pre-dawn signal: light not yet broken. |
| **VECTOR** | Deep blue gradient: royal blue to navy | Calculated divergence in the structural depths. |
| **GRAVITON** | Muted gold/bronze gradient, lighter top to darker base | Mass settling under its own weight. |
| **EQUILIBRIUM** | Warm brown/copper gradient, medium earth to richer base | The grounded register of the heavyweight. |
| **BURNER** | Deep red/crimson gradient: vibrant top to near-black bottom | The descending arc of late-stage failure. |
| **BOT** | Muted purple/violet gradient: dusty grey-purple to deep plum | Mechanical neutrality, off spectrum. |
| **PASSENGER** | Muted olive/army green gradient: dark khaki to drab green | Earth-tone baseline. |

#### Visual Intuition

The vocabulary is designed to be **parseable without reference**:
* A holder seeing a **deep red gradient** knows they are looking at a **BURNER** before reading a single word.
* An **iridescent surface** immediately signals the mathematical coherence of an **ANOMALY**.
* **Color carries the first reading; text confirms it.**

This visual system ensures that the rarity and behavioral "vibe" of a Star are felt viscerally by the collector, reinforcing the emotional weight of the prediction-market archive.

## 3.6 Rendering Architecture

The Card is generated through a hybrid composite pipeline. Its visual surface is assembled from two structurally distinct elements: a deterministic SVG framework and a generative raster image; and rendered to a single flattened PNG. The PNG is the Card as anyone encounters it: on the PS surface, on external marketplaces, in token metadata, in any context where the Card is displayed.

#### The Generation Pipeline

The pipeline operates in three stages to ensure that every Card is both structurally precise and visually consistent.

* **Stage 1 — Deterministic SVG Framework.** This is the structural surface of the Card. It uses a fixed backend SVG template that remains identical across all Archetypes. The framework injects:
    * **Typographic content:** Wallet Address, Entry Bracket, behavioral readouts, signature, and dossier into native `<text>` nodes.
    * **Tier-driven colors:** Stroke and fill properties applied to the frame and separators via the **Propagation System**.
    * **Sector colors:** Dynamically assigned hex codes for the SECTOR tag.
    * **Seasonal accents:** Genesis cyan, Standard grey, Singular amber, Fractal blue, Origin pink, or Looter green.
* **Stage 2 — Generative Raster Imagery.** The central event artwork is generated as a PNG by a generative model conditioned on the event's title and description. To maintain high quality, each image is curated by a team member. Recurring "Fractal" events reuse cached imagery to maintain series consistency.
* **Stage 3 — Composite Flattening.** The SVG framework and the raster artwork are composited and rendered into a **single flattened PNG**. This PNG is the final artifact submitted to token metadata and displayed across all platforms.

#### Why Flattening?

The hybrid approach (SVG + Raster) produces a Card with crisp typography and evocative imagery. However, live SVG rendering is notoriously inconsistent across external platforms like OpenSea or Blur—marketplaces often strip fonts or fail to render embedded images.

> **Unification is deliberate.** By using a flattened PNG, PS eliminates visual asymmetry. A collector sees the exact same artifact on the PS audit page as they do on a secondary marketplace. The Card looks the same wherever it is encountered.

#### The SVG as Production Substrate

The SVG framework is an internal production tool, not a user-facing layer. Its value lies in its ability to inject per-Card data into a deterministic template, producing consistent results across all thirteen Archetypes and seasonal contexts.

**Permanence:**
* Once flattened, the visual surface is **locked**.
* The PNG captures the Card exactly as it appeared at the moment of mint.
* While future protocol versions may evolve the visual depth (texture, density) within an Archetype’s color family, the **base color signature** remains fixed for the duration of v1.

| Component | Format | Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Structure/Text** | SVG | Precision, data injection, and geometric logic. |
| **Event Art** | PNG (Raster) | Visual recognition and curated generative aesthetics. |
| **Final Artifact** | PNG (Flattened) | Universal compatibility and immutable visual state. |
